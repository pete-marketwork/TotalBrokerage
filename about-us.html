Our story

Built for brokers,
by brokers.

TotalBrokerage was founded by two University of Florida graduates who understood real estate from both sides — operations and technology. They built the platform they couldn't find.

How it started

A college friendship. A shared problem.

Michael Mosseri and Ben Schachter became fast friends as students at the University of Florida. Ben built one of the most successful multi-state brokerage firms in the country. Michael became an architect-level software engineer and CEO. When the time came to transform how brokerages operate, the path was clear: they would build it together.

Why TotalBrokerage

Ben needed it. Couldn't find it. So they built it.

While running his brokerage, Ben struggled to find software that met his needs. So he partnered with Mike. With Ben's operational experience and Mike's engineering depth, they founded TotalBrokerage in 2015. Today it powers hundreds of brokerages and tens of thousands of transactions every year.
